# Family swarm personas

These are the practical user types the site is now serving more directly.

## 1) U.S. remote-working couple, children 4 and 7
- Goal: test Bali before a bigger move
- Pressure points: budget, area choice, school rhythm, housing timing
- Best entry pages:
  - `/test-stay`
  - `/guides/moving-to-bali-with-primary-age-kids`
  - `/budget-calculator`

## 2) Australian family with teens
- Goal: see if Bali could work for a term or longer
- Pressure points: teen buy-in, commute, social fit, daily structure
- Best entry pages:
  - `/guides/moving-to-bali-with-pre-teens-and-teens`
  - `/guides/bali-areas-for-teens`
  - `/schools`

## 3) Japanese family with children 3 and 6
- Goal: calm family rhythm, not chaos
- Pressure points: routines, food, safety, support, area choice
- Best entry pages:
  - `/guides/moving-to-bali-with-toddlers`
  - `/settling-in`
  - `/areas`

## 4) Korean family considering a one-term stay
- Goal: more real than a holiday, lighter than relocation
