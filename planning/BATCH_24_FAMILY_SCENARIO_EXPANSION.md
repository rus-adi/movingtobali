# Batch 24 — family scenario expansion

## Goal

Make the site feel bigger and more personal by giving families more specific entry points than age alone, without drifting into thin duplicate content.

- `src/components/FamilyPathMatcher.tsx`

## Site surfacing updates

- family paths pillar expanded
- homepage family-setup section widened
- `Plan Your Move` tool grid expanded
- `Decision Checklists` gets a family-shape cluster
- footer and nav logic updated for the new tool
- contact and sidebar pathways updated to surface family-path tools earlier

## Strategic reason

This batch makes the site feel more premium because it stops pretending every family is deciding the same move.

It also gives the user more ways to enter the system before they are ready for a school decision, while still keeping Empathy School as the educational anchor when that question becomes relevant.
