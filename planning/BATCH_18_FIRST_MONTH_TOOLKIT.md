# Batch 18 — First Month Toolkit

## Goal
Make the site feel more substantial by adding one strong interactive tool plus supporting pages around the most emotionally loaded stage of the move: the first week and first month in Bali.

## What shipped
- New interactive route: `/first-month-planner`
- New component: `src/components/FirstMonthPlanner.tsx`
- New guide: `content/guides/first-month-in-bali-with-kids.md`
- New guide: `content/guides/how-to-plan-your-first-week-in-bali-with-kids.md`
- New resource: `content/resources/first-week-arrival-checklist.md`
- New resource: `content/resources/family-routine-reset-sheet.md`
- Homepage updated to surface the new planner and month-one content
- Navbar / footer updated to expose the planner

## Why this batch matters
Families often do not need more generic Bali content. They need help with the first month, because that is where:
- housing pressure rises,
- school questions become real,
- transport and errands stop being abstract,
- exhaustion makes bad decisions more likely.

This batch creates a clearer bridge between inspiration and real family planning.
