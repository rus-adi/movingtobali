# QA test session checklist

## First impression
- Does the homepage immediately explain what the hub is?
- Is it obvious that this belongs to Empathy School?
- Is it clear that Gaia Group is the housing partner?
- Does the homepage feel broad but still organized?

## Navigation
- Can you find Search quickly?
- Can you tell the difference between Plan, Areas, Housing, Daily Life, and Empathy School?
- Does the mobile menu feel clear enough?

## Homepage flows
Try each of these from the homepage:
- plan the move
- compare areas
- find housing support
- understand Empathy School fit
- browse by family setup
- watch video recaps

## Search
Try searches for:
- Ubud
- rent deposit
- school tour
- rainy season
- Gaia Group
- Empathy School
- a nonsense phrase

Check:
- results quality
- no-results recovery
- speed / clarity

## Content pages
Open at least one:
- area page
- guide
- resource
- blog post

Check:
- breadcrumbs
- sticky sidebar
- next steps block
- trust links
- related content quality

## Tools
Smoke-test:
- Area Match
- Compare Areas
- Commute Reality
- Budget Calculator
- First Month Planner
- Housing Intro Readiness
- Housing Brief Builder
- Empathy School Fit
- Empathy School Tour Prep
- Weekday Reality

## Contact / conversion
From at least three different pages, check:
- contact route feels appropriate
- prep links make sense
- form feels calm and clear
- Gaia Group intro route feels gated enough
- Empathy School inquiry route feels natural

## Dead-end recovery
Test:
- 404 page
- empty search
- wrong search term
- random outdated internal link if available

## Visual polish
Check:
- spacing consistency
- button consistency
- hover states
- image cropping
- badges and chips
- footer usefulness
- overall tone: warm, premium, human
