# Post-Batch-30 growth map

Batch 20–30 built the core system.
What comes next is not “just keep adding pages.”
It is a tighter, more selective growth phase.

## Priority 1 — real proof layer
When approved and available:
- direct parent quotes
- short parent case studies
- founder / team voice clips
- school-tour reflections from real families

## Priority 2 — owned media expansion
- more school recap pages
- more daily-life clips and recap pages
- more housing process explainers
- test-stay walkthrough recaps

## Priority 3 — performance and analytics refinement
- inspect which bundles actually convert
- inspect where families drop off before contact
- tighten CTA placement by route intent
- test homepage module order

## Priority 4 — selective pruning and refreshing
- refresh older early pages that now feel thinner next to the newer system
- combine overlapping pages where needed
- keep area and visa content reviewed on cadence

## Priority 5 — premium operational additions
Only if genuinely useful:
- downloadable starter pack bundle
- printable family move board PDF
- richer compare-page interactions
- saved shortlist / saved planner state

## Guardrails
- Empathy School remains the only promoted school
- Gaia Group remains the only public housing partner unless strategy changes
- no thin pages for the sake of volume
- no fake testimonials
- no directory sprawl
