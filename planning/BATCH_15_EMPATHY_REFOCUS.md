# Batch 15 — Empathy School refocus + expansion roadmap

## Why this batch exists
The hub is now being treated as a product of Empathy School, not a neutral school-comparison site.

That changes the architecture:

- The only school we actively promote is **Empathy School**
- Multi-language rollout is **not** in scope yet
- Housing support is intentionally narrow, with **Gaia Group** as the preferred partner
- The site needs to feel bigger through **clearer pathways and stronger pages**, not a pile of thin posts

## What ships in this batch
### 1) Navigation + homepage refocus
- Navigation now exposes:
  - Start Here
  - Test Stay
  - Areas
  - Visas
  - Housing
  - Costs
  - Empathy School
- Homepage now pushes:
  - test-stay path
  - housing path
  - budget calculator
  - settling-in path
  - Empathy School anchor path

### 2) New pillar pages
- `/test-stay`
- `/settling-in`

### 3) Schools page rewritten as an Empathy School anchor
- `/schools` now works as:
  - fit + tour + routine page
  - commute-aware school anchor
  - internal conversion path to Empathy School
- We removed the broad compare-the-island framing.

### 4) Housing partner integration
- Gaia Group added as the public housing partner
- Partner directory narrowed to verified items only
- Housing page now includes a disclosed Gaia Group intro box

### 5) Practical tooling
- Added `/budget-calculator`
- Added:
  - housing viewing scorecard
  - test stay decision scorecard
  - housing-agent questions guide
