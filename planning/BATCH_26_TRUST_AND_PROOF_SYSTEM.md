Make the site feel more lived-in and more credible without inventing testimonials or turning the hub into a noisy partner funnel.

## What this batch adds

- Trust / disclosure polish across homepage, plan hub, Gaia Group, Empathy School fit, FAQ, partners, footer, and sitemap

## Core rule used in this batch

Do not publish fake parent testimonials.

Until direct parent quotes are gathered and approved, use **composite family scenarios** and label them clearly.

## Strategic effect

- stronger trust without pretending certainty
- bigger-feeling site through proof and depth, not filler
- better bridge from content into real decisions
- clearer explanation of why the public partner layer stays narrow
