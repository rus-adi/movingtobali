# Batch 17 — conversion polish, topic routing, and Gaia trust layer

## Goal
Make the site feel more actionable without bloating it:
- better contact routing by user intent,
- stronger conversion paths from the biggest pillar pages,
- a clearer and more trustworthy Gaia Group housing flow,
- practical Empathy School pages that support the move rather than acting like a generic schools directory.

## What this batch adds

### Contact experience
- Topic-based contact routing with 5 starting points:
  - General move planning
  - Test stay plan
  - Area + budget question
  - Housing intro
  - Empathy School fit
- Topic-specific prompts and “what to include” guidance on the contact page.
- Better preservation of `from` and `partner` context in contact links.

### Conversion polish on major pages
- Contextual sidebar CTA cards on major pillar pages and selected guides.
- Page-specific actions instead of one generic “contact us” block everywhere.

### Gaia Group trust layer
- Expanded Gaia Group partner profile with:
  - trust points,
  - process steps,
  - good-fit / not-for-you guidance.
- New housing-support explainer guide.
- New family housing brief template so intro requests are more useful.

### Empathy School move-planning pages
- New guide: using an Empathy School tour to test the real week.
- New resource: Empathy School commute + routine test sheet.
- Internal links from Schools and Test Stay back into these pages.

### Budget tool polish
- Added 3-month runway and 12-month view to make the calculator feel more planning-oriented.
- Updated budget page CTA to route into the new area + budget contact path.

## Why this matters
The site now feels less like a static content library and more like a guided decision system:
- users can identify their situation faster,
- page CTAs are more specific,
- Gaia Group is positioned with clearer trust boundaries,
- Empathy School is integrated as a real anchor in the move.

## Likely next batch
- stronger homepage blocks for “Ask by topic” and “Work with Gaia Group”
- page-level trust/credibility polish (founder quotes, process visuals, calmer testimonial framing)
- more practical tools for settling-in and first-month logistics
