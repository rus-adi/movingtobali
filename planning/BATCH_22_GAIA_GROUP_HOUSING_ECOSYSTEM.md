## Why this matters

The housing path is one of the highest-stakes parts of the site.
If it stays too thin, the whole hub feels smaller than it really is.

This batch makes housing feel like its own decision system:
- a partner profile,
- a readiness filter,
- a message builder,
- stronger educational content around home type and fit.

## UX rules reinforced

- Gaia Group remains the only public housing partner.
- The route stays shortlist-first, not directory-first.
- The site should help families know whether they are ready, not push them into intros too early.
- The copy should sound grounded and parent-led, not salesy.
