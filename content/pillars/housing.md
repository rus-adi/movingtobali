## Quick start (the fast version)

- Shortlist **2–3 areas first**. Pace + commute + noise matter more than villa photos.
- If you can, do a **short stay**, view places in person, then sign.
- Treat deposits as high-risk moments: verify identity, contract, and what’s included.

## The housing approach that keeps families safe

In our experience, the safest pattern is:

1) **Short stay**  
2) **View in person**  
3) **Verify + negotiate**  
4) **Pay deposits only after clarity**

## Step 2: Decide whether you need a partner

Not every family needs an agent.

A partner is often most useful when:
- you are moving on a tighter timeline,
- you want help coordinating viewings,
- you need a calmer first shortlist,
- you want support close to the Empathy School side of Bali.

If that sounds like you, Gaia Group may be a helpful fit.
If not, the checklists on this page are enough to keep the process safer.

Before you request an intro, do three small things:
- copy the [Family housing brief template](/resources/family-housing-brief-template),
- compare the likely [housing styles](/housing-style-compare),
- read [How Gaia Group housing support works](/guides/how-gaia-group-housing-support-works).

That keeps the first conversation grounded in real family needs instead of vague wishlist energy.

---

## Step 3: What to verify before you pay anything

Verify:
- **who owns the property** or who legally represents the owner,
- **what’s included**,
- **what happens when something breaks**,
- **deposit terms**,
- **payment schedule**,
- **receipts**.

Use:
- [Lease + deposit checklist](/resources/lease-deposit-checklist)
- [Lease + deposit questions](/guides/lease-and-deposit-questions-in-bali)

---

## Step 4: The family comfort walkthrough

When you view a place, do a quick parent scan:
- mold + airflow,
- water pressure,
- internet reality,
- noise check,
- safety basics,
- sleep setup,
- how the place feels at ordinary-child volume.

Use:
- [Housing viewing scorecard](/resources/housing-viewing-scorecard)

---

## Step 5: Commute is the hidden cost

In Bali, commute affects:
- how calm mornings feel,
- whether kids can still do afternoon activities,
- whether parents can work without feeling stretched.

If Empathy School is part of the plan, use that commute as a real test.

Related:
- [Empathy School](/schools)

## What we learned the hard way

The biggest housing stress is not “finding something.”

It is paying money before clarity.

If you keep one rule, keep this one:
**Don’t let urgency override verification.**
