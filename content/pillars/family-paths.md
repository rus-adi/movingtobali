## Quick start

- Start with [Family path match](/family-path-match) if more than one guide sounds relevant.
- Choose **one primary path** and **one secondary lens**. That is usually enough.
- Read the page that sounds most like your real family, not the family you wish you were on your best day.
- Use the path to decide what matters first: **routine, area, housing, school, or move tempo**.
- If you still feel broad and undecided, begin with [Start here](/start-here).

## How to use family paths well

The goal is not to find one universal answer.

The goal is to stop reading advice that belongs to a different family.

Usually the strongest approach is:

1. choose the path that sounds most like your real family,  
2. add one secondary lens if needed,  
3. let those two pages change the next decisions,  
4. ignore the rest for now.

## What kind of paths usually combine well

### Child stage + adult reality

Examples:
- toddlers + remote-working family
- primary-age kids + structure-first family
- teens + slower relocation path
- single parent + 1–3 month stay

### Child temperament + move tempo

Examples:
- slower-to-warm-up child + slower relocation path
- highly active child + short stay with real outlets

### School question + family pattern

If Empathy School becomes part of the move, let that sit on top of the family path rather than replacing it.

That means:
- remote-working families still need work rhythm,
- single parents still need a smaller daily loop,
- slower-to-warm-up children still need trust and repetition,
- structure-first families still need a more readable week.
