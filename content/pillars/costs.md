## Quick start (the fast version)

- Build **ranges**, not perfect spreadsheets. Bali pricing varies.
- The biggest budget drivers are usually **housing + learning + transport**.
- Decide your family’s “tradeoffs”: space vs location, driver vs scooters, eating out vs cooking.
- Use the [Budget calculator](/budget-calculator) for a first working range.
- Include a buffer for deposits + setup. The first month is rarely average.

## The 4 budget drivers that matter most

### 1) Housing
Housing cost is not just rent. It is also:
- deposit size and return terms,
- what’s included,
- how much your area choice affects transport and energy.

Useful tools:
- [Housing](/housing)

### 2) Learning
Learning can be a major budget line and a major reason families choose Bali.

Transport changes both budget and stress.

## Build a realistic budget range in 20 minutes

This is the method we recommend because it still works when prices move.

1) Choose your **two target areas**  
2) Pick a housing style  
3) Decide your transport default  
4) Decide your learning plan  
5) Add healthcare and setup buffer  
6) Use the [Budget calculator](/budget-calculator)

If you prefer a copy/paste worksheet:
- [Family budget range planner](/resources/family-budget-range-planner)

## Hidden costs families commonly miss

- **Deposits + upfront payments**
- **Setup week spending**
- **Commute time**
- **Visa/entry costs**  
- **Convenience spending when everyone is tired**

Official sources:
- [Official links](/official-links)

## What we learned the hard way

Trying to “solve the budget” before you have chosen areas usually creates false precision.

A calmer path is:
- shortlist areas,
- do a test stay,
- then lock in housing and learning once daily life is clearer.
