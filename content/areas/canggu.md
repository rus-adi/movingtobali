## Quick take

Canggu is the high-choice, high-friction version of Bali family life: exciting for some families, draining for others.

## Who usually keeps Canggu on the shortlist

- Families who enjoy having a lot happening around them.
- Adults who value convenience, social options, and work-friendly environments.
- Older kids or teens who may appreciate more movement and activity nearby.

## What the family week tends to feel like

Canggu can feel energising because there is always something to do. The tradeoff is that the area rarely disappears into the background. The family week often feels fuller, louder, and more decision-heavy than in quieter parts of the island.

## Why this area keeps working for some families

- There is strong choice density for food, movement, and social life.
- It can be easier to build a routine quickly when many services already exist.
- Families who dislike feeling remote may feel reassured here.

## Tradeoffs to be honest about

- Traffic can turn a theoretically fine plan into a tiring one.
- Kids who need lower sensory load may fatigue faster.
- Parents sometimes mistake familiarity for fit because the area feels easy for adults but heavy for the whole week.

## Who probably should not start here

If your family keeps using words like calm, gentle, slower, or smaller, Canggu may be better as a visit than a base.
