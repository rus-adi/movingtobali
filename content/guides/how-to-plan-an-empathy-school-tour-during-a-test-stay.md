## Quick take

A tour inside a test stay is usually the best version of the school question.

Why?
Because Empathy School stops being a standalone appointment and starts behaving like part of a real family week.

Use the stay to test:
- the route,
- the family’s energy,
- the area logic,
- and whether the school makes the move feel more grounded.

## The timing that often works best

### Too early can be noisy

In the first couple of days, the family is still recovering from travel and setup.
That can make the school day harder to read.

### Too late can waste the rest of the stay

If you wait until the final day, the tour may give you a useful answer too late to change:
- the area shortlist,
- the housing viewings,
- or the rest of the planning week.

### A calmer middle usually works

A good pattern is:
1. land,
2. let the family have one or two ordinary days,
3. visit Empathy School,
4. use the answer to shape the rest of the stay.

## What to protect on the school day

- enough sleep the night before,  
- a realistic start from your actual base,  
- snacks and water if the child is joining,  
- enough space afterwards to notice the real feeling,  
- and one ordinary task after the visit so the day does not stay in “tour mode.”

## What not to stack onto the visit

Usually avoid combining the school day with:
- multiple villa viewings,
- a packed sightseeing day,
- heavy arrival-week logistics,
- or the family’s most stretched work day.

The goal is not to make the day impressive.
It is to make it truthful.

## The best question to answer afterwards

Before the family moves on, write one honest sentence:

**If Empathy School became real, what would we now change about the move?**

That answer might be:
- the area shortlist,
- the housing brief,
- the timing,
- or the fact that school should be treated as a stronger anchor than before.

## When to repeat the route

If Empathy School may become one of the main anchors, repeat the route at least once.
Especially if:
- you are staying farther away,
- you are testing a rainy or busier week,
- or the commute already feels like it might narrow your choices.

## What we learned the hard way

A school day during a test stay works best when it is allowed to change what happens next.

If the visit cannot influence the rest of the stay, families often leave with a nice impression but a weaker decision.
