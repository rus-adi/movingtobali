## Quick take

A test stay is not just a smaller version of a full move.

It changes the quality of the later decisions.

It is usually the better starting point when:
- the area is still unclear,
- the family is split on Bali,
- Empathy School is still only a maybe,
- or you know the move needs more reality before it needs more commitment.

## Start with the uncertainty level

The core question is not:
“Which move shape sounds bolder?”

It is:
“How much uncertainty is still expensive for us to carry?”

If the answer is “a lot,” a test stay or trial stretch is usually kinder.

## When a test stay tends to win

A test stay often wins when:
- you still need to compare areas honestly,
- the child’s response to Bali matters more than your own,
- you want to see whether Empathy School changes the move,
- you need the first decisions to stay reversible.

## When a fuller move may still make sense

A fuller move may make sense when:
- timing is already decided,
- the family has strong signal,
- the school question is already much clearer,
- or the strain of staying in limbo feels worse than committing.

## Where Empathy School belongs in the decision

If Empathy School is going to shape the move, let that question enter before you harden housing.

A tour, a test of the route, or a more grounded school-fit conversation can change:
- where you focus the move,
- whether a shorter trial is enough,
- and how much certainty you really have.

## The common mistake

Families sometimes choose the full move because they are tired of uncertainty.

But unresolved uncertainty does not disappear just because the commitment got bigger.

It usually shows up later through:
- housing stress,
- area regret,
- or a school decision that entered too late.
