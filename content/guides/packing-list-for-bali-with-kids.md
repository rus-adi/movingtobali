## Quick take

Most families do better when they pack for the **first week**, not for every possible Bali scenario.

## Pack first for function

### Carry-on essentials
- passports and entry documents,
- one change of clothes per child,
- medicines you really use,
- chargers, comfort item, snacks, and anything that protects the first tired evening.

### First-week basics
- lightweight clothes that can repeat,
- simple rain layer,
- sun protection,
- sandals or shoes that can get wet,
- swimwear only if it supports your real week, not just the fantasy version.

### Child regulation items
Bring the small familiar things that help your child reset: headphones, preferred cup, bedtime object, one book, one easy activity.

## Things families often overpack

- too many “just in case” outfits,
- bulky toys instead of a few reliable comfort objects,
- items they could easily buy after arrival.

## What is worth protecting

The smartest packing list protects:

- sleep,
- first-day hydration and meals,
- basic health needs,
- and emotional familiarity for the children.
