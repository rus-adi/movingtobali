## Quick take

Not every contact should sound the same.
A family asking broad relocation questions needs a different reply from a family choosing between Ubud and Sanur, or a family deciding whether Empathy School should anchor the move.

That is why the site now uses five conversation routes:
- [Plan the move](/contact?route=planning&topic=General%20move%20planning)
- [Plan a test stay](/contact?route=test-stay&topic=Test%20stay%20plan)
- [Areas + budget](/contact?route=areas-budget&topic=Area%20%2B%20budget%20question)
- [Housing intro](/contact?route=housing-intro&topic=Housing%20intro)
- [Empathy School fit](/contact?route=empathy-school&topic=Empathy%20School%20fit)

If you pick the route that matches the real decision, the reply gets better faster.

## Start with the decision, not the form

A lot of families hesitate before contacting because they assume they need to explain everything.
That is usually not the problem.

The real problem is that the question is still too broad.

A cleaner way to think about it is:

**What answer would make the next step clearer this week?**

If that answer is about order, sequencing, or what matters first, you probably need [general move planning](/plan-your-move).
If that answer is about geography and cost, you probably need [areas + budget](/budget-calculator).
If that answer is about whether school should shape the move, you probably need [Empathy School fit](/empathy-school-fit).

## Route 1: plan the move

Use this when the move still feels wide.
You may be serious about Bali, but the family still needs help deciding what belongs now, what belongs later, and what can wait.

This route is usually right when:
- you still need the order of decisions,
- you are still choosing between test stay and bigger move,
- several issues feel tangled together,
- or the family has one large sticking point but no clean sequence yet.

## Route 2: plan a test stay

Use this when Bali already feels plausible, but the family wants a lower-risk way to gather signal.
A good test stay is not a vacation with better branding.
It is a structured way to learn whether the move still feels good once the week becomes real.

This route is usually right when:
- you want to test two or three areas,
- you want to see whether [Empathy School](/schools) belongs in the picture,
- you want to pressure-test the first month before committing harder,
- or the adults want enough evidence to say yes or no calmly.

## Route 3: areas + budget

Use this when Bali has started to shrink into a real shortlist and the family needs help making tradeoffs visible.
This is not only about price.
It is about how area, transport, school distance, convenience, and adult energy change the cost of the whole week.

This route is usually right when:
- you are narrowing areas,
- commute pressure is changing the shortlist,
- the budget feels possible in one area and tight in another,
- or Empathy School may affect the geography of the move.

Useful prep:
- [Area Match](/area-match)
- [Compare areas](/compare-areas)

## Route 4: housing intro

Use this when the move has enough shape that Gaia Group can actually help.
A housing intro works best when it narrows the search.
It works badly when the family is still asking Bali to be everything at once.

This route is usually right when:
- you have a real timing window,
- two or three areas feel serious,
- the budget band is usable,
- and the family knows what the home must do for the week.

Useful prep:
- [Housing intro readiness](/housing-intro-readiness)

## Route 5: Empathy School fit

Use this when the school question is no longer casual.
That does not mean you are ready to enroll.
It means school may now be strong enough to change area, commute, and the family week.

This route is usually right when:
- school could anchor the move,
- you want to plan a tour or test the day around a tour,
- the commute to school might change housing logic,
- or you want to know whether Empathy School belongs in a test stay.

Useful prep:
- [Empathy School fit](/empathy-school-fit)

## When you are between two routes

That is normal.
Here is a useful tie-breaker:

- If the whole move still feels tangled, choose **plan the move**.
- If Bali feels plausible but unproven, choose **test stay**.
- If the big question is geography and cost, choose **areas + budget**.
- If you are already thinking about partners and intros, choose **housing intro**.
- If school might change everything else, choose **Empathy School fit**.

## The honest rule

The strongest conversation is usually the one that makes the *next* decision easier.
Not the one that tries to solve the whole move at once.

If that is still fuzzy, use [conversation paths](/conversation-paths) or go straight to [contact](/contact).
