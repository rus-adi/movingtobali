## Quick take

Gaia Group is not meant to be a giant public property directory.

It is most useful when a family wants a calmer housing process:
- fewer but more relevant options,
- a shortlist shaped by family routine,
- and support that stays close to the Empathy School / Ubud-side rhythm of Bali.

If your plan is still wide open, do the early thinking first.
If your dates, budget, and area direction are already taking shape, an intro can save time.

---

## What Gaia Group is good for

In practical terms, Gaia Group tends to help most with:
- shaping a first shortlist,
- coordinating viewings,
- keeping the conversation grounded in family reality,
- helping families avoid the classic mistake of chasing villa photos before they understand the area.

That matters because most housing stress in Bali is not about a lack of listings.
It is about mismatch.

A house can look perfect online and still be wrong for your week.

## What to prepare before you request an intro

Try to have these ready:

### 1) Likely dates

Not perfect dates.
Just a real window.

Examples:
- arriving for a 3-week test stay in July,
- moving in September if the test stay goes well,
- coming for viewings at the end of the school holiday.

### 2) Bedroom reality

How many bedrooms do you actually need?
How will sleep work?
Will grandparents visit?
Will one room double as a work room?

### 3) Budget band

Not one magic number.
A real range.

Examples:
- "We want to stay under this if possible."
- "We can stretch to this for the right place."

### 4) Area direction

Not all of Bali.
Usually two or three areas.

If Empathy School is part of the move, say that clearly.
It changes what “reasonable” looks like.

### 5) Non-negotiables

For example:
- calmer road situation,
- enclosed outdoor area,
- easier school-run route,
- fewer stairs,
- room for two children to sleep separately.

## How the process usually works

### Step 1: You send the brief

The strongest intro requests are short and specific.
A simple message is enough if it includes:
- dates,
- likely areas,
- bedroom needs,
- budget band,
- and whether Empathy School is shaping the move.

Use this if you want a head start:
- [Family housing brief template](/resources/family-housing-brief-template)

### Step 2: Fit check

We keep the intro route warm on purpose.
That means checking whether Gaia Group is actually the right fit for your timing and needs before the conversation starts.

### Step 3: Shortlist or viewing plan

If the fit is right, the next useful step is usually a shortlist or a viewing plan.
Not a flood of random properties.

### Step 4: You verify everything before committing

This part matters.
Even with a trusted partner, families should still verify:
- who owns the property or legally represents it,
- what is included,
- the payment schedule,
- what happens if something breaks,
- what the contract actually says.

Use:
- [Lease + deposit checklist](/resources/lease-deposit-checklist)
- [Housing viewing scorecard](/resources/housing-viewing-scorecard)

## When Gaia Group is probably a good fit

Gaia Group is usually most helpful when:
- you are doing a test stay and want viewings to count,
- you want housing support near the Empathy School side of Bali,
- you prefer fewer stronger options over endless browsing,
- you want the housing conversation to reflect how your family actually lives.

---

## When it is probably too early

It is probably too early if:
- you still have no idea which part of Bali interests you,
- your budget is still completely open-ended,
- you are still treating the move like a holiday fantasy,
- you want to pay before viewing or verifying.

That is not a problem.
It just means you will get more value from:
- [Start here](/start-here)
- [Areas](/areas)
- [Budget calculator](/budget-calculator)
- [Test stay](/test-stay)

## The part families should not outsource

A good partner can reduce friction.
They should not replace your judgement.

The final yes still needs to come from you.

Ask yourself:
- Can I picture our real mornings here?
- Does the route still feel okay after a tired pickup?
- Does the contract feel clear enough to sign calmly?

If the answer is still no, slow down.
