## Rain does not ruin Bali, but it changes the week

The mistake is not underestimating the weather.
The mistake is underestimating how weather changes **family energy**.

Rainy season can affect:
- travel time,
- what children can do outside,
- whether laundry dries,
- how quickly everyone gets cabin-feverish,
- how much patience the adults still have by late afternoon.

The calmer answer is not to fight the season. It is to build a slightly different week.

---

## Build rainy-day defaults before you need them

The best rainy-day plan is boring on purpose.
Know these in advance:
- one indoor play default,
- one easy snack or meal plan,
- one transport backup,
- one quieter evening version of the day.

That way the rain changes the plan without stealing the whole day.

---

## Expect the commute to feel heavier

The same drive can feel very different in rain.
So can the same pickup.

That is why rainy season is such a useful honesty test for an area.
If the family already feels stretched by transport, wet weather often makes the real answer easier to see.

## Protect regulation, not productivity

Parents sometimes try to compensate for bad weather by overfilling the indoor day.
Usually that backfires.

What helps more:
- shorter expectations,
- easier transitions,
- more snacks and hydration,
- one or two reliable indoor activities,
- accepting that the day may feel narrower.

If children are already tired, the goal is steadiness, not output.

---

## Make the house do more work

In rainy weeks, the house matters more.
Ask yourself:
- is there enough floor space to play,
- is there a good quiet corner,
- where do wet shoes and bags go,
- where does drying laundry actually live,
- can the adults still function when everyone is inside longer than planned?

That is practical housing information, not a small detail.

---

## Use rain as a routine test, not a failure

A rainy day can tell you a lot:
- whether the area is still workable,
- whether the home setup helps,
- whether your afternoon rhythm is too ambitious,
- whether you need easier defaults for food and downtime.

This is useful information, even when the day is not your favorite.
