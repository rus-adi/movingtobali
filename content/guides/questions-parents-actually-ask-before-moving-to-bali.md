## Why this guide exists

There are lots of Bali questions.
The problem is not a lack of questions.
It is a lack of **order**.

These are the kinds of questions parents keep returning to when the move starts to feel real.
Use them to make the next decision better, not to make every decision now.

## Questions to ask before you plan anything bigger

### What shape of move are we actually making?

Are you:
- still exploring,
- planning a test stay,
- using Empathy School as an early anchor,
- or already moving fast enough that housing feels urgent?

Until that is clear, research tends to sprawl.

### What would make this move feel kind to our family?

Not exciting.
Not impressive.
Kind.

That might mean:
- fewer long drives,
- calmer mornings,
- slower first commitments,
- or a school rhythm that stabilizes the week.

## Questions to ask before a test stay

### What are we trying to prove or disprove?

A test stay works best when it has a job.
Examples:
- narrow two areas to one,
- see whether Empathy School belongs in the plan,
- test whether the adults still like the idea on an ordinary weekday,
- or learn whether a short stay should become a longer trial.

### Which ordinary moments are we protecting?

Try to keep:
- one normal weekday,
- one school-related visit if it matters,
- one grocery or practical errand block,
- one route test,
- and one evening when everyone is a little tired.

Those moments often teach more than the highlight reel.

## Questions to ask before choosing an area

### What will Thursday feel like here?

That is often more helpful than asking which area is “best.”

### Are we choosing an area or a weekly radius?

A weekly radius includes:
- school routes,
- food,
- work,
- rainy-day defaults,
- and how many long drives the family can tolerate before it changes everyone’s mood.

## Questions to ask before Empathy School enters the plan

### Could school change the rest of the move?

If the answer is yes, then school belongs early.
Empathy School may change:
- area choice,
- commute tolerance,
- first-month housing,
- or even whether the move should stay staged.

### Are we trying to answer the right school question?

The strongest question is usually not:
**“Do we like the school?”**

It is:
**“Do we like our family life around this school?”**

## Questions to ask before housing gets serious

### Do we have enough signal for a useful shortlist?

Before asking for help or falling in love with a property, it helps to know:
- likely dates,
- likely budget band,
- realistic areas,
- bedroom needs,
- commute tolerance,
- and whether Empathy School is shaping the week.

### Are we choosing a home or a system?

The right answer is always the second one.
A house touches:
- transport,
- school,
- tired evenings,
- groceries,
- maintenance,
- support,
- and total budget reality.

## Questions to ask before you call the move a success

### Is the family calmer, clearer, or kinder than before?

That is a better success signal than whether every detail is fixed.

### What are we still allowed not to know yet?

Healthy moves keep some things open until ordinary life creates better evidence.
That is not a flaw.
That is usually wisdom.
