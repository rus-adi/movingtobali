## Written answer first

Beautiful Bali housing clips are useful.
They are also dangerous.

They can show:
- light,
- style,
- a mood,
- a rough sense of space.

They cannot show:
- how loud the road feels at pickup time,
- whether the airflow is actually good,
- whether the route to school gets old quickly,
- what the place feels like when everyone is tired,
- or whether the house fits your family **in motion**, not just in stillness.

That is why this page exists.

---

## What reels are good for

### 1) Vibe and direction

A clip can help you decide whether you want more:
- greenery,
- beach-side buzz,
- residential calm,
- or a smaller daily radius.

That is useful.

### 2) Knowing what attracts you

Sometimes a video helps you see what you keep responding to.
Maybe it is quiet.
Maybe it is light.
Maybe it is outdoor space.

Those preferences matter.
They just should not make the decision alone.

---

## What reels hide from you

### Sound

A calm-looking clip can sit on a road that feels completely different in real life.

### Heat, airflow, and mold

These are family-life issues, not aesthetic issues.
And they often matter more than the sofa.

### The radius of the week

A villa can be beautiful and still wrong because the daily radius is bad.
If groceries, school, pickups, and work all stretch too far, the move gets heavy quickly.

### Maintenance and support

Videos do not tell you how issues get handled.
That still has to be asked in plain language.

## The parent question to ask after every housing clip

Not “Do I like it?”

Ask this instead:

**Would this house make our real week easier?**

That includes:
- the school run,
- grocery loop,
- noise tolerance,
- child sleep,
- adult work windows,
- and how much driving the family can absorb without getting brittle.

---

## A better sequence than saving endless reels

1. Shortlist areas first.
2. Decide whether Empathy School is part of the weekly map.
3. Build the housing brief.
4. Then start looking at actual homes.

That sequence protects you from letting style outrun clarity.
