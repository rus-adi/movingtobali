## Written answer first

Primary-age decisions often feel deceptively easy.

A school can look warm, active, and interesting — and still not fit the child, the commute, or the family week.

This recap helps you watch the Primary tour for the things that actually matter:
- does the environment support growing independence,
- do adults feel steady,
- and would the school make the move feel more organised rather than more stretched.

---

## What matters most here

### Independence with support

Primary years are often where parents start looking for both freedom and containment.

A useful tour makes you feel that balance.
Not total looseness. Not over-control. A real middle.

### Rhythm

The question is not whether the day seems “busy.”
It is whether it seems coherent.

Would your child move through this day in a way that builds confidence, or in a way that leaves them scattered?

### Weekly effect

Primary school choices shape:
- where you live,
- how long mornings take,
- what pickup feels like,
- how much margin is left for the rest of family life.

So watch this video with the whole week in mind.

---

## What to do after watching

If the school feels more likely:
- narrow the area shortlist with the commute in mind,
- protect time around the tour,
- and avoid making the school a background question if it is clearly moving to the front.
