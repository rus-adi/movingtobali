## Written answer first

A good school tour should make the move **clearer**, not just more exciting.

That is why this recap is not really about whether the campus looks nice.
It is about whether the tour changes three practical questions:

- should Empathy School anchor the move,
- should it change your area shortlist,
- and would the weekly rhythm still work once the novelty wears off.

If the video helps with those questions, it is useful.
If it only makes the school feel charming, it is not enough.

---

## What the tour is really telling you

### 1) Campus flow

Watch how spaces connect.

Not in a “beautiful campus” way.
In a parent way.

Could a child move through this day without feeling jangled, over-handled, or lost?

### 2) Adult presence

Parents usually learn more from tone than from information.

Do the adults seem grounded?
Do they look like people you would trust when a child is tired, dysregulated, or having an ordinary difficult day?

### 3) Outdoor rhythm

Empathy School’s environment matters.
But what matters more is how the outdoor energy feels in relation to your child.

For some families, this is the reason the school feels obvious.
For others, it reveals that they still need more time or a different pace.

### 4) Weekly effect

A school does not sit in isolation.

If this school feels likely, it changes:
- your housing radius,
- your commute tolerance,
- your pickup energy,
- and sometimes the entire emotional shape of the move.

That is why this video belongs in a relocation hub, not only on a school page.

---

## What to watch in the first minute

If you only have a short window, pay attention to:

- whether the space feels calm or merely quiet,
- whether adults seem relational,
- whether you can imagine your child here on a completely normal day,
- whether your body says “this could work” or “I still can’t picture it.”

That last answer matters more than any polished talking point.

---

## What this video cannot answer for you

A tour video can help.
It cannot decide for you.

It will not tell you:
- how your child will feel after three weeks,
- what the school run does to the week,
- whether Ubud-side living genuinely suits your family,
- or whether your adult bandwidth is strong enough for this version of the move.

## A better next step than overthinking the video

If the video makes Empathy School feel more likely, do not keep watching more clips without a plan.

Instead:
1. decide whether school fit should enter the move now,
2. protect enough time around the tour day,
3. use the school to test the week, not just the campus.
