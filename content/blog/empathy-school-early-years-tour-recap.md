## Written answer first

Early Years fit is often decided by things that are easy to miss in a normal tour.

Parents of younger children are usually trying to answer quieter questions:

- would my child feel held here,
- would mornings feel safer or more strained,
- and does the emotional temperature of the space match what my child needs.

That is what this video is good for.

---

## What to watch for

### 1) Emotional temperature

Does the space feel warm enough for small children?

Not just attractive.
Warm.
Held.
Regulated.

### 2) Transition support

Young children live inside transitions.
Arrival, snack, movement, toileting, shifting attention.

If those moments look thoughtful, it is a good sign.
If they look rushed, parents usually feel it quickly.

### 3) Scale and sensory load

A younger child may love a nature-rich environment and still get tired by the overall stimulation.

Watch for:
- visual busyness,
- sound,
- movement,
- and whether the space feels expansive in a soothing way or a draining way.

### 4) Your own body response

This is more useful than it sounds.

After watching, do you feel your shoulders drop a little?
Or do you still feel a low-level “I’m not sure.”

That signal matters.
