## Why this small video belongs on the hub

Not every useful relocation asset is a checklist.

Some are tiny bridges that help children feel that Bali is becoming familiar rather than merely new.

## What the clip helps with

### Lowering pressure
Children do not need a full cultural briefing on day one. A song can do more than a lecture because it meets them at a level they can actually enjoy.

### Building repetition
The more something repeats kindly, the less foreign it feels.

### Creating respectful curiosity
A small cultural touchpoint can open the door to better questions later: what did you notice, what felt fun, what sounded different?

## Use this well

Keep it light. Then connect it back to daily life, school, or a cultural moment with humility.
