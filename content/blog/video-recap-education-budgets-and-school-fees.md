## The useful point of this short

The clip is helpful because it stops families from pricing Bali as if education were a side note.

On this hub, the school question means Empathy School, tours, camp, and how learning changes the rest of the week.

## What the video is really saying

### Education is a swing factor

If Empathy School becomes part of your move, the budget question is no longer just tuition or fees. It can also change:

- where you live,
- how much transport you need,
- what the afternoon feels like,
- and how much flexibility remains elsewhere in the month.

### Ask for the full picture early

The video points families toward a better habit: do not budget from one headline number alone.

Ask for the full structure and then test it against the likely weekly reality.

### Use ranges first

The short is also a reminder that low / mid / high planning is kinder than a single brittle number.
