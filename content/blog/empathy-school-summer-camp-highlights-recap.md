## Written answer first

The useful question is not whether the camp looks fun.

It is whether the camp would make your family’s week **easier**.

For many families, camp matters because it gives children immediate structure while the adults handle housing, transport, errands, or school questions.

That is why this recap is part of the move system.

---

## What this video can tell you

### Daily rhythm

Does the day look held together?

Camp is most useful when it gives children a rhythm they can trust.
That rhythm is often more valuable than any individual activity.

### Supervision you can feel

Parents are not looking for cinematic beauty.
They are looking for adult presence.

Watch transitions, movement, and how the day seems to regroup itself.

### Child fit

Some children light up in group energy and outdoor movement.
Others need more predictability, shade, or a quieter pace.

A highlight reel cannot answer fit completely, but it can narrow the question.

---

## Why this matters during a test stay

When families land in Bali, the week fills up quickly.

Camp can create:
- a child anchor,
- some social ease,
- and a little adult breathing room for housing viewings or practical setup.

That does not make camp mandatory.
It makes it one of the most useful optional supports in the system.
