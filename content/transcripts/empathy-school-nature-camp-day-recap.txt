Detailed recap (not verbatim)

Video: “Nature camp: what a day can feel like”

This is not a policy document. It is a viewing guide for parents.

What to pay attention to

1) Energy level
- Does the day feel expansive but contained?
- Could your child handle this much outdoor time and movement?

2) Adult supervision
- Look at transitions, group moments, and edges of activity.
- The goal is not constant interference. It is adult presence you can feel.

3) Child fit
- Nature camp can be wonderful for some children and tiring for others.
- Ask whether your child tends to regulate through movement and outdoor space, or whether they need more shelter and predictability.

4) Usefulness during a test-stay
- Camps help when parents need time to test housing, areas, paperwork, and daily logistics.
- The question is not only whether the camp looks fun. It is whether it would make your week calmer.

Questions to ask after watching
- Would this help our child feel grounded quickly in Bali?
- Would this give us enough space to handle housing and admin during a test-stay?
- What safety and communication details do we still need in writing?
