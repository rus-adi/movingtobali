Detailed recap (not verbatim)

Video: “How we run an Empathy School tour”

This is the parent version of the video — not a shot-by-shot transcript. The point is to turn the tour into a decision tool.

What the tour is really answering

1) Does the campus energy feel calm enough for your child?
- Notice the pace of transitions.
- Notice whether children seem rushed or settled.
- Notice whether the environment looks performative or lived-in.

2) Do adults feel present in a real way?
- Look for adult attention during transitions, not just during the talking points.
- Notice whether support feels relational or merely supervisory.

3) Would this school make your week easier or harder?
- Imagine your real morning, not your best-case morning.
- Imagine pickup time, work calls, transport, and how much buffer your family actually has.

4) Does the school match your child’s style?
- Some children soften quickly in nature-rich, community-led environments.
- Some need more structure, more time, or a different pace.

Questions to answer after watching
- Can I picture my child here on an ordinary Tuesday?
- Would this school change which area we should live in?
- Is the commute still acceptable once we picture pickup, work, and a tired child?
- Do we need a tour next, or are we not at that stage yet?
