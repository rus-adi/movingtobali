Detailed recap (not verbatim)

Video: “Empathy School Early Years tour”

This recap is built for parents evaluating fit, not for marketing.

What to watch for

1) Emotional temperature
- Does the space feel warm and grounded?
- Do younger children appear held by the environment rather than managed by it?

2) Transition support
- Early years fit often shows up in tiny moments: arrival, moving between spaces, snack, toileting, rest.
- Look for whether those moments appear rushed or gently guided.

3) Scale and sensory load
- Notice noise, movement, and visual stimulation.
- Ask yourself whether your child would likely regulate here or fatigue quickly.

4) Outdoor flow
- Notice how outdoor space is used.
- The useful question is not whether it looks beautiful. It is whether it feels safe, age-appropriate, and repeatable every day.

Questions to answer after watching
- Would my child settle more easily in a smaller, calmer environment like this?
- What kind of arrival support would my child need?
- Would this campus rhythm fit our family mornings?
