Detailed recap (not verbatim)

Video: “Empathy School Primary tour”

What this clip helps you judge

1) Independence with support
- Primary years often work best when children have room to move and explore, with adults still visibly steering the day.
- Look for whether that balance feels real here.

2) Daily rhythm
- Does the day look coherent?
- Do spaces connect naturally, or does the environment feel fragmented?

3) Learning atmosphere
- The useful question is not “Does this look impressive?”
- It is “Would my child stay curious, connected, and well-supported in this kind of rhythm?”

4) Family-week effect
- Primary school decisions change pickup energy, housing radius, and afternoon bandwidth.
- Watch the video with your weekly logistics in mind, not just the campus tour lens.

Questions to answer after watching
- Would my child be more open or more overwhelmed in this environment?
- Does this make Ubud-side life more likely for our family?
- Would the school-run still feel worth it after two months, not just on day one?
