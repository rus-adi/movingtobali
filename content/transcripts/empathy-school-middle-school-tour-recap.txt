Video: “Empathy School Middle School tour”

What middle-school parents should actually look for

1) Adolescent dignity
- Do the spaces and adult interactions feel age-respectful?
- Notice whether older students seem treated like emerging young people, not bigger small children.

2) Voice and independence
- Look for signs of agency, ownership, and room to think.
- Ask whether your child would feel invited into the culture or merely expected to adapt.

3) Pace and belonging
- Middle school fit often depends less on glossy programs and more on whether a child feels socially safe and mentally engaged.
- Watch for whether the environment looks pressured, flat, or alive.

4) Weekly reality
- Older children often care deeply about commute, social energy, and whether life feels too isolated or too busy.
- Use the video to picture the whole week, not just the tour hour.

Questions to answer after watching
- Would my child feel seen here?
- Is the surrounding lifestyle compatible with this stage of family life?
- Would we need a longer test-stay before deciding?
