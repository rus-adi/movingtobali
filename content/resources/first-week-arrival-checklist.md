## Use this checklist for the first 7 days

### Family basics
- [ ] Everyone has enough water, snacks, and simple breakfast options.
- [ ] You know where basic groceries come from.
- [ ] You have one calm dinner fallback for tired evenings.
- [ ] You know where the nearest pharmacy or clinic option is.

### Phones and payments
- [ ] You have working internet / SIM setup.
- [ ] You have a backup payment method.
- [ ] You saved key addresses in maps.

### Transport
- [ ] You tested the easiest short trip with children.
- [ ] You know whether your family prefers walking, ride-hail, driver, or some combination.
- [ ] You tested one route at a realistic family time, not just the easiest hour.

### Area fit
- [ ] You walked or drove the area in the morning.
- [ ] You checked what late afternoon / evening feels like.
- [ ] You noticed noise, rain drainage, traffic, and family convenience.

### Housing
- [ ] You are clear whether your current place is just a landing pad or a serious option.
- [ ] If you are viewing homes, you are using a real scorecard rather than memory alone.
- [ ] You are not rushing into certainty just because everyone is tired.

### Learning
- [ ] You decided whether to test [Empathy School](/schools) this week.
- [ ] If school matters, you are thinking about commute and family rhythm, not only philosophy.

### Family energy
- [ ] At least one afternoon stayed intentionally light.
- [ ] You noticed who in the family is coping well and who is close to overload.
- [ ] You protected one familiar ritual from home.
