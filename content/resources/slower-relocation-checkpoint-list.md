## Checkpoint 1 — after the first test stay

Write down:
- which area felt most livable,
- what still felt heavy,
- whether Empathy School moved forward or stayed in the background,
- what the family would test differently next time.

## Checkpoint 2 — before you ask for housing help

Confirm:
- your likely dates,
- your top areas,
- budget range,
- whether the move is a test or a longer stay,
- what the home actually needs to support.

## Checkpoint 3 — before school becomes “solved”

Ask:
- did we test the commute honestly?
- are we responding to real fit or only a polished first impression?
- does the school make the week easier?

## Checkpoint 4 — before bigger commitments

Make sure these still feel true:
- the area shortlist still makes sense,
- the family week is getting easier to picture,
- the adults are not using exhaustion to force certainty,
- the move still has a useful next step rather than a rushed one.
