## Use this when

You are down to two Bali areas and both still sound plausible.

The goal is to compare:
- morning tone,
- school / commute reality,
- groceries and practical life,
- evening calm,
- and whether the area still feels worth its tradeoffs.

## Compare the real week

### Morning route
- Which area asks for more buffer?
- Which area feels easier to leave from?
- Which area would still be okay on a rough morning?

### Empathy School question
- If Empathy School matters, which area keeps that question easier?
- Which area makes the school feel like a real anchor instead of a long-drive hope?

### Practical life
- groceries
- pickup / drop-off flow
- walkability or driver dependence
- tired-afternoon errand reality

### Family energy
- Which area protects the child better?
- Which area protects the adults better?
- Which area still feels kinder by evening?

### Budget pressure
- Which area would quietly increase convenience spending?
- Which area gives more room to stay flexible longer?

- “If we choose Area B, the week gets harder because…”
