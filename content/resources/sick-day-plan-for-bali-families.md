## What this plan should answer in advance

- Who decides whether it is a clinic day or a home day?
- Who handles transport and admin?
- What information do you need immediately?
- What makes the child more comfortable while adults are deciding the next move?

## Keep one simple sick-day protocol

1. Slow the day down.  
2. Check hydration, temperature, and how the child is presenting.  
3. Open the clinic / insurance / location info quickly.  
4. Decide who is with the child and who handles the admin.

## Helpful items to keep ready

- thermometer,
- child medicines you already know how to use,
- electrolytes or hydration support,
- spare clothes,
- charger,
- medical summary note.
