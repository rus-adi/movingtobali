## Use this list to protect the real questions

### Campus feel
- Does the atmosphere feel calm, rushed, or overly performative?
- Would my child likely soften here or brace here?

### Adult presence
- What do transitions look like on an ordinary day?
- How do adults support children who need more time or reassurance?

### Family-week effect
- How would this school change our area choice?
- Would the commute still feel acceptable after pickup, rain, or a harder day?

### Fit and next step
- What would you suggest we notice during a test stay?
- What kind of family usually settles well here?
- What should we clarify before making the next decision?
