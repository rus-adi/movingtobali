## How to use this

Copy this into your notes app before a tour, a school conversation, or a test-stay day.

The goal is to leave with an answer that affects the move.
Not just a vague good feeling.

## Copy / paste notes

### Before the visit

- Child age(s):
- Our likely move shape:
- Areas still in the shortlist:
- What we hope school would help with most:
- What we are still worried about:

### During the visit

- First feeling on campus:
- What adult presence felt like:
- What kind of child seemed comfortable there:
- One thing that felt easier than expected:
- One thing that felt heavier than expected:

### Commute and day feel

- Where we started from:
- How the drive felt on the way in:
- How the family felt after the visit:
- Would this route still feel okay repeatedly?
- If Empathy School became real, what would change about area or housing?

### The honest sentence

Complete this sentence before you leave the day behind:

**If Empathy School became part of our move, our week would feel:**

### Next move

- [ ] School should anchor the move more strongly now.
- [ ] We need a sharper area shortlist first.
- [ ] We need a second visit or conversation.
- [ ] Empathy School stays in the picture, but not as the main anchor yet.
