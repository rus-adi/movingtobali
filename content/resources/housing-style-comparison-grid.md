## Use this when

You are not just choosing a listing.
You are choosing what kind of housing should carry this stage of the move.

Compare:
- temporary / landing stay,
- standalone villa / family house,
- managed or serviced home.

## Score each style from 1–5

### Protects flexibility
Can this option hold uncertainty without forcing commitment too early?

### Supports real routines
Would it help the family live normally, not just stay comfortably?

### Keeps setup light
How much admin or maintenance does this create right now?

### Fits the area truth
Does this style match what you actually know about the area and route so far?

### Budget honesty
Does this help the budget stay believable for this stage?

## Questions to write underneath

- What stage of the move are we actually in?
- Are we asking this housing style to solve uncertainty it cannot solve?
- Does this option protect area learning, or hide it?
- If Gaia Group helps, what would we want them to optimize for first?
