## Copy/paste test sheet

Use this during a test stay or school tour day.
The goal is not to score the school as a product.
The goal is to test the whole week.

### Before the visit
- Where are we staying?
- What time did we leave?
- Traffic feel on the way there:
- Did the trip already change our mood / energy?

### During the visit
- First emotional reaction to the campus:
- Could we picture our child here?
- What felt calm / grounded?
- What felt uncertain?

### The school-run reality
- Does this route feel sustainable once it is normal, not special?
- Would this still feel okay in rain / tired mornings / pickup traffic?
- What would we probably need to change in housing if this became the plan?

### After the visit
- Energy level on the way back:
- Could we still do normal life afterwards (groceries, work, sibling needs, rest)?
- Did the return trip make the routine feel more realistic or less realistic?

### One honest sentence
- If Empathy School became part of our move, our week would feel:

### Final note
- What is the next decision this visit clarified?
