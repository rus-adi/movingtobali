## Use this when

Empathy School is moving from abstract idea to real decision.

This grid helps you judge whether an area still works once the school route becomes part of:
- mornings,
- pickups,
- adult work windows,
- and how much family energy is left by late afternoon.

## Score each area from 1–5

### Morning calm
How likely is this area to protect a steady school morning?

### Route predictability
Does the route feel repeatable or fragile?

### After-school energy
Would the child and adults still have margin after the return?

### Adult work protection
Can work still happen without the route dominating the whole day?

### Housing compatibility
Does the housing radius still make sense if Empathy School matters more?

### Long-term honesty
Does the area still feel like a good idea once you imagine repeating this route for real?

## Questions to write under the score

- What part of the school route feels easiest here?
- What part of the school route feels most fragile?
- If we chose this area, what would housing need to optimize for?
- Are we keeping the area because it truly works, or because we like the story of it?

## Best use of the result

A lower score does not automatically eliminate an area.
It tells you what the area is costing.

Then you can decide whether that cost is worth paying.
