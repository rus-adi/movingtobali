## Use this planner like a family, not like a school assignment

Write short answers. Be honest. If a question feels annoying, it is probably useful.

### 1. Morning reality
- What time does the family need to be moving?
- What has to happen before that?
- What part of the morning already feels fragile?

### 2. Transport reality
- Which drive matters most?
- How much commute friction can your children really absorb?
- What is your rainy-day backup?

### 3. Food reality
- What are the three breakfasts that always work?
- What is the easy dinner plan on tired days?
- What happens when groceries are not sorted?

### 4. Child anchor
- Is there one repeating anchor this week: school, camp, class, or family routine?
- If not, what would give the child repetition fastest?

### 5. After-school reality
- What happens in the first 45 minutes after pickup?
- Does the child need quiet, movement, or food first?
- What adult task should **not** happen during that window?

### 6. Sick day reality
- Which clinic would you use?
- Who handles transport?
- Where are the important documents?

### 7. Rain reality
- What is the indoor default?
- What part of the day becomes hardest when the weather turns?
- What do you want to simplify on wet days?
