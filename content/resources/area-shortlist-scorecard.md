## How to use this

Print or copy one section per area.

Do not score the holiday version.
Score the weekday version.

If Empathy School might be part of the move, let that commute count.

---

## Area 1

Area name:

### Score each 1–5

- Pace / calm
- Morning commute reality
- Grocery + essentials loop
- Walkability / easy errands
- Fit for your children’s ages
- Parent energy after a normal day
- Budget pull (does this area push you higher than planned?)
- Would you still choose it in heavy rain?
