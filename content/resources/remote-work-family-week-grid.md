## What this grid should reveal

A remote-work family does not only need internet and desks. It needs a week where adult focus time and child regulation can coexist.

## Fill in the week with honesty

For each weekday, note:

- adult focus windows,
- child anchors,
- transport needs,
- lunch / meal friction,
- likely low-energy moment,
- backup plan when the day slides.

## Watch for these patterns

- work calls stacked against pickup,
- a home layout that looks good but does not support concentration,
- an area choice that makes every afternoon heavier,
- too many moving parts for the adults carrying the schedule.
