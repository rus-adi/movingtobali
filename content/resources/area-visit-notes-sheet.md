## Test these honestly

### Groceries / essentials

- Was the errand loop easy?
- Would we still feel fine doing this weekly?

### Food rhythm

- Did meals feel easy enough with kids?
- Did we rely on convenience more than we want long term?

### Rest + sleep

- How did naps, downtime, or evening wind-down feel here?
- What happened after dark: noise, mosquitoes, road stress, overstimulation?

### Commute reality

- What would a normal school / activity run feel like from here?
- If Empathy School is part of the move, did that make the area feel more or less logical?
