## Use this as a sample day, not a rigid script

The point is to keep enough space around the visit that the day still feels real.

## Sample plan

### Night before

- confirm the start time,
- keep dinner and bedtime simple,
- charge phones,
- prepare a notes app or paper sheet,
- pack water and snacks if the child is joining.

### Morning

- leave from your actual base,  
- leave early enough that the drive is believable but not frantic,  
- notice what the family mood is before you arrive.

### During the visit

- watch more than you speak,  
- ask fewer, sharper questions,  
- notice adult energy, transitions, and campus flow,  
- imagine your actual child there, not an easier imaginary version.

### Right after

- do not rush straight into multiple property viewings,
- sit somewhere quiet for 10–20 minutes,
- write your first honest sentence about the day,
- decide what the visit changed.

### One normal task afterwards

Add one ordinary family task if possible:
- lunch,
- groceries,
- a simple drive back,
- or another everyday stop.

That is often where the truth shows up.

## What not to do

- do not stack the day too hard,  
- do not use arrival exhaustion as your only signal,  
- do not leave without notes,  
- do not act like the campus and the commute are separate decisions.
