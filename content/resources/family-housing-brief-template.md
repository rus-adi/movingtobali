## Copy/paste template

Use this before you request a housing intro.
The goal is not to be perfect.
The goal is to be specific enough that the shortlist becomes useful.

---

Hi,

We are planning a Bali move / test stay and would like help with housing.

### Timing
- Likely arrival window:
- Are we doing a test stay first?
- When would we want viewings / move-in?

### Family setup
- Adults:
- Kids’ ages:
- Any visiting family / nanny / helper setup we should plan for?

### Bedrooms + house setup
- Ideal number of bedrooms:
- Minimum number of bedrooms:
- Work-from-home needs:
- Sleep setup details that matter:

### Budget band
- Comfortable monthly range:
- Stretch range for the right place:
- What we do **not** want to pay for:

### Area shortlist
- First-choice areas:
- Backup areas:
- Why these areas feel right so far:

### School / routine anchor
- Is Empathy School part of the plan?
- If yes, how strongly should commute shape the shortlist?
- If no, what will shape the daily routine instead?

### Non-negotiables
- Example: calmer road situation
- Example: enclosed outdoor area
- Example: easier family commute
- Example: fewer stairs

### What we are trying to avoid
- Example: too much traffic friction
- Example: paying before we understand the area

## What makes this more useful

A strong brief usually includes:
- a real date window,
- a budget band instead of one magic number,
- two or three areas instead of “all Bali”,
- honest non-negotiables.

That is enough to make the first conversation much better.
