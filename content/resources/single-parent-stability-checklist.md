## What stability means here

For a single parent, stability usually means the week is small enough, supported enough, and repeatable enough that one hard day does not knock everything over.

## Check the core layers

- area radius,
- transport backup,
- food defaults,
- child anchor,
- adult support or community touchpoints,
- housing that reduces rather than adds friction.

## Strong questions

- If I get sick, what is the backup?
- If transport changes suddenly, what is plan B?
- Does this area still work if I do not have much margin that day?
- Is this school / housing / activity choice making the week lighter or heavier?
