## Use this before you hit send

You do not need a perfect brief.
You do want the first message to include enough real signal that Gaia Group can be helpful.

## Checklist

- [ ] I know our likely arrival or scouting window.
- [ ] I know whether this is a test stay, a trial move, or a fuller move.
- [ ] We have narrowed Bali to **two or three serious areas**.
- [ ] We have a **comfort range** and a **stretch ceiling** for housing.
- [ ] We know our likely bedroom count and any work-from-home needs.
- [ ] We can say whether **Empathy School** is shaping the shortlist.
- [ ] We know our non-negotiables.
- [ ] We know the dealbreakers that would quietly ruin the week.
- [ ] We are prepared to verify the property, terms, and payment flow before money moves.

## What to include in the first message

Keep it short:
- timing,
- move shape,
- kids’ ages,
- bedrooms,
- budget band,
- shortlist,
- non-negotiables,
- whether Empathy School is part of the housing logic.
