## How to use this

Use this when the move needs shape.
Not every item must be complete.
The point is to see what belongs in the next 30 days, what should wait, and what should stay soft until reality is clearer.

---

## Next 30 days

Focus on decisions that reduce uncertainty fast.

- Decide whether this is a test stay, staged move, or full move.
- Use [Area Match](/area-match) to narrow Bali to 2–3 real options.
- Build a first-pass range in the [Budget calculator](/budget-calculator).
- Decide whether Empathy School needs to be part of the early planning.
- Use the [Family readiness checklist](/resources/family-readiness-checklist).

## 30–60 days

Focus on decisions that become useful once the shape of the move exists.

- Pressure-test your area shortlist.
- Plan the test stay or first month.
- Decide whether to request a Gaia Group housing intro.
- Prepare school-tour questions if Empathy School is part of the move.
- Review the visa path and what still needs confirming through official sources.

## 60–90 days

Focus on the pieces that make more sense once the family has real signal.

- Refine housing expectations.
- Decide what the first month should test.
- Confirm arrival-week logistics.
- Protect the family’s first routines.
- Decide which commitments are finally ready to harden.

## The question for each stage

### Next 30 days
What would reduce the biggest uncertainty for our family?

### 30–60 days
What is real enough now to deserve more structure?

### 60–90 days
What still needs protecting from premature commitment?
