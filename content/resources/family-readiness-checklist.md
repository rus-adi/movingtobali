## How to use this

Do not use this to pressure yourselves into a yes.
Use it to see whether the move has enough shape to become useful.

Answer each with:
- Yes
- Not yet
- We need to talk about this

---

## Family clarity

- We can say **why** Bali is attractive for our family, not just why it sounds exciting.
- We can say what we are hoping changes in our weekly life.
- We can name the biggest risk or hesitation honestly.
- We know which adult is more ready and which adult still needs clarity.
- We know how our children tend to respond to change, routine shifts, and long days.

## Timeline clarity

- We have a realistic window for a test stay or arrival.
- We know whether we are thinking in weeks, months, or longer.
- We know whether a staged move would feel better than a full commitment.

## Budget clarity

- We have a rough low / mid / high range in mind.
- We are not relying on one perfect housing number.
- We understand that the first month often costs more than later months.

## Area clarity

- We know that choosing an area changes almost everything else.
- We are willing to shortlist two or three areas instead of trying to love all of Bali at once.
- We know what we value most: calmer pace, easier routines, convenience, walkability, school commute, or something else.

## School clarity

- We know whether school needs should shape the move early.
- If Empathy School is in the picture, we are ready to treat it as a real decision anchor rather than a vague idea.
- If school is not the first decision, we know what will anchor the week instead.

## Housing clarity

- We do not expect online listings alone to solve the move.
- We understand that housing gets safer when area and family needs are clearer.
- We are willing to verify before we pay.

## What to do with the result

### If most answers are “Yes”

You probably have enough shape to move into planning mode.
Start here:
- [Plan your move](/plan-your-move)
- [Move timeline](/move-timeline)

### If most answers are “Not yet”

Do not panic.
It usually means the move is still too broad.
Narrow the next step instead of trying to solve everything.

### If the answers are mixed

That is normal.
Choose the one question most likely to reduce the whole family’s uncertainty.
Often that is:
- area,
- timeline,
- budget posture,
- or whether a test stay is the right next move.
