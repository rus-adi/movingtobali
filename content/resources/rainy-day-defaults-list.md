## Copy this into your notes app

### Indoor defaults
- One activity children can do without a long setup.
- One calmer option for a low-energy child.
- One parent-friendly option when adults still need to function.

### Food defaults
- Easy snack ready immediately.
- One warm or comforting meal that does not take much thought.
- Hydration covered before anyone gets grumpy.

### Transport defaults
- Who calls the driver or app?
- What route becomes the backup route?
- What outing is no longer worth it in rain?

### Home defaults
- Where do wet shoes, bags, and towels go?
- Where does laundry dry now?
- Which room becomes the recovery space?

### Evening defaults
- Earlier dinner if the day already feels heavy.
- Lower stimulation.
- Tomorrow prepared in five minutes, not a full reset project.
