## Fill this in before the week begins

### Pickup plan
- Who handles pickup?
- Is the route home direct?
- What one extra stop are we **not** doing?

### Food plan
- What snack is waiting?
- What is the easiest dinner if the child is already tired?
- What adult task can wait until after food?

### Regulation plan
- Does this child usually need quiet, movement, connection, or alone time first?
- What will make that easier today?

### Evening plan
- What time do we want the house to start getting softer?
- What needs to be ready for tomorrow?
- What can be skipped if everyone is done?
