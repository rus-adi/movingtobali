## Use this after a test stay

Rate each from 1–5 and add one sentence of evidence.

### Area
- Morning energy
- Noise at night
- Walkability
- Rainy-day practicality
- Family feeling

### Housing
- Could we picture ordinary life here?
- Would the commute work?
- Does the home style fit our real budget?
- Did viewings make the move feel easier or harder?

### Learning
- Did we tour Empathy School?
- Could we picture the routine?
- Did the school commute feel realistic?
- Did this part of the move feel lighter after seeing it?

### Parent reality
- Workability
- Transport stress
- Food / grocery rhythm
- Healthcare confidence
- Overall nervous system score

### Final outcome
Choose one:
- Yes, this move still feels right
- Maybe, but only with changes
- Not now

Then write the most important reason.
