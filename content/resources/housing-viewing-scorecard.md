## Copy/paste scorecard

Use a quick 1–5 rating for each home you view.

### Area fit
- Morning commute
- Night noise
- Walkability
- Grocery convenience
- Overall family feeling

### House basics
- Bedrooms work for actual sleep
- Airflow / mold check
- Bathrooms feel usable
- Water pressure
- Internet reality

### Kid reality
- Stairs / balconies
- Pool safety
- Gate / outdoor boundary
- Nap or quiet space
- How the house feels at tired-child volume

### Contract + money
- Ownership / representation clear
- Deposit terms clear
- What’s included is written down
- Maintenance responsibility is clear
- Receipt process is clear

### Final note
Write one sentence:
**Would we still like this house after three ordinary Tuesdays?**
